VoiceVault

your iPhone voice is kinda mid? same. here's how to give VoiceOver a glow-up with free open voices. takes like 5 minutes. no cap.

Note: psst, why Piper? Piper adds voices to your WHOLE iPhone, not just one app. So VoiceOver can use them, and so can Speak Screen and friends. No Piper means no fancy voices, sorry bestie. Piper needs iOS 18 or later. Piper not installing? Your iPhone is probably too old school, so update iOS first (steps below).

  1. Get Piper. It's free. Get Piper on the App Store
  2. Open Piper and download a voice. Piper is the engine, not the voice, so it shows up empty at first. Listen to a few samples in the app, then download the one that hits.
  3. Pick your voice for VoiceOver. Go to Settings, then Accessibility, then VoiceOver, then Speech. Tap the name of your current voice, then tap Voice, and choose your new Piper voice.
  4. Can't decide? Use Add Rotor Voice in that same Speech screen to keep more than one and swap with the rotor. indecisive kings and queens rise.
Update steps
  1. Plug your iPhone in and connect to wifi. Apple also says back it up first.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap General.
  4. Tap Software Update.
  5. If an update shows up, tap Download and Install. Then go touch grass while it installs.

Apple Support: update your iPhone or iPad

Once your voice is installed

your new voice is not just a VoiceOver thing. Apple says voices from apps like Piper work with VoiceOver and with Speak Screen too. Both of these go to Apple Support.

  • For VoiceOver
  • For Spoken Content (Speak Screen and Speak Selection)
